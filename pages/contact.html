FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Here are some common questions we receive.

How long does a project take?
Project timelines vary based on complexity. A typical AI website takes 2-4 weeks, while complex automation systems take 4-8 weeks. We'll provide a timeline during consultation.
What's your payment structure?
We require 50% deposit to start, 25% on progress review, and 25% upon completion. All payments in Naira (₦) via bank transfer.
Do you provide post-launch support?
Yes! All projects include 30 days free support. We also offer affordable maintenance packages for ongoing support and updates.
What AI solutions do you offer?
We offer custom AI model development, chatbots, predictive analytics, business automation, and intelligent systems tailored to your specific needs.
Can you help with SEO and growth?
Yes! We include basic SEO optimization in all websites. Advanced SEO and digital marketing packages are available for businesses wanting to grow faster.
Can I update the website myself?
Yes! We provide CMS solutions (WordPress, custom admin panels) that allow you to easily update content without technical knowledge.
